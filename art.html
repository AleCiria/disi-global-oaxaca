Art at DISI

Contemporary art created in collaboration

We invite artists to bring creative practices into conversation with diverse intelligences, collective inquiry, and the ecological and cultural context of Oaxaca.

Call for artists

Create, experiment, and think with the DISI community

DISI Global Oaxaca invites artists from different disciplines and stages of their careers to participate in an immersive gathering from February 15 to 26, 2027, at ENES Unidad Oaxaca, UNAM. Artists will work alongside researchers, students, activists, practitioners, and storytellers to explore ecological and situated intelligence through contemporary artistic practice.

Rather than arriving with a predetermined final product, participants will be encouraged to develop ideas through exchange, experimentation, and collaboration. Artistic processes may respond to the questions that emerge during DISI, to the local context, or to the relationships between biological, social, cultural, and artificial systems.

Ways of participating

Practices that can meet and transform one another

01

Collective creation

Develop work with other participants, allowing different forms of knowledge and practice to shape both the process and its outcomes.

02

Situated practice

Engage carefully with Oaxaca's ecological, social, and cultural context, recognizing that place is not a backdrop but part of the inquiry.

03

Art, science, and other knowledges

Create exchanges across artistic research, science, technology, community knowledge, and forms of lived experience.

04

Open formats

Propose installations, performances, sound or moving-image work, participatory actions, workshops, visual narratives, or other contemporary forms.

What to propose

An idea with room to change

Applications should describe a question, process, or initial artistic idea that could grow through collaboration during DISI Global Oaxaca. Proposals should explain how the practice connects with ecological or situated intelligence, collective creation, and the context of the gathering.

Please also indicate the format, approximate duration, materials, spatial or technical needs, and possible ways for other participants to become involved.

Collaboration is central. The proposal does not need to define a finished artwork. We are interested in practices that remain open to listening, experimentation, shared authorship, and the possibility of becoming something different through the encounter.
