Organizers

DISI Global Oaxaca is organized by DISI Fellows and Faculty from the Research Institute in Applied Mathematics and Systems (IIMAS), UNAM.

Gabriel Ramos-Fernández

Senior Researcher · DISI Fellow and DISI Faculty

Gabriel Ramos-Fernández is a full-time Researcher in the Department of Mathematical Modeling of Social Systems at the IIMAS, UNAM. He holds a B.Sc. in Basic Biomedical Research and a Ph.D. in Biology from the University of Pennsylvania, and has held research stays at UNAM's Institute of Physics, the Ecotono Laboratory at the University of Comahue, and the Global Research Centre for Diverse Intelligences at the University of St Andrews. His research focuses on the emergence of global properties in biological and social systems, especially collective information processing, using tools such as social interaction observation, network analysis, mathematical modeling, and simulations. He is also interested in socio-ecological systems and biodiversity conservation.

Sandra Elizabeth Smith Aguilar

Postdoctoral Researcher · DISI Fellow

Sandra Elizabeth Smith Aguilar is a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Mathematical Modeling of Social Systems at the IIMAS, UNAM. Sandra, is a biologist trained at UNAM, where she also earned a master's degree in Marine Sciences and Limnology. She holds a Ph.D. in Conservation and Use of Natural Resources from CIIDIR Oaxaca, Instituto Politécnico Nacional. Her research focuses on animal behavior, including humpback whale song and the social behavior of spider monkeys. Since 2013, she has integrated complex systems approaches and social network analysis into her work. She is a DISI Fellow, and coordinates the Oaxaca activities of the Sociedad de Científicos Anónimos.

Alejandra Ciria

Researcher · DISI Fellow

Alejandra Ciria is a full-time researcher in the Department of Computer Science at the IIMAS, UNAM. She holds a B.Sc. in Psychology, a master's degree in Cognitive Sciences, and a Ph.D. in Experimental Psychology. Head of the Embodied Cognition Lab (Laboratorio de Cognición Corporizada, LabCoCo), originally based at the School of Psychology, UNAM, where she was a full-time professor in Cognitive and Behavioral Sciences from 2020 to 2025. The lab integrates multidisciplinary approaches to study how the body and situated action cycles, grounded in the physical properties of the environment, shape cognitive processes in biological and artificial agents. Her research focuses on embodied cognition + artificial intelligence, and the computational modeling of cognitive processes in biological and artificial systems.

About DISI

The Diverse Intelligences Summer Institute

DISI is a community-building initiative exploring "the origins, nature, and future of intelligences", diverse manifestations of mind and cognition, of agency and awareness, across species and systems. It brings together early-career scholars and storytellers, deliberately breaking down traditional disciplinary boundaries through transdisciplinary lectures, seminars, projects, meals, and salons.

DISI is held annually at the University of St Andrews, Scotland. In 2026, DISI was also held for the first time in the United States, at Hobart and William Smith Colleges in New York's beautiful Finger Lakes region. DISI has also grown a global network of DISI Global events that carry its community-first ethos into new ecological and cultural contexts around the world, of which this Oaxaca edition is one.
